## Methodology notes
- **What the denominator is varies by school.** Most schools report % of graduates *accepting* jobs: Wharton, Fuqua, Kellogg, Tepper, McCombs, Foster, Darden and Stanford ("percent accepting"). MIT's industry table does not state its base. Booth, Yale and UCLA report % of *hires*. Columbia reports % of *offers* and includes sponsored students. A school that counts only job-seekers who accepted will show a higher share than one that divides by the whole class.
- **Industry definitions vary.** Haas uses "Technology / Telecom", Stern "Technology/Telecommunications" and Cornell "Technology & Telecommunications". Others report "Technology" with sub-industries. Some schools put FinTech under Finance and others under Tech: Wharton lists FinTech separately at 1.5%, and Booth counts Financial Technology inside Technology.
- **Standards.** Wharton, Haas, Stanford and Tepper each state that they collect data per **MBA CSEA (Career Services & Employer Alliance) Standards**. Haas notes that *internship* data is **not** governed by CSEA standards. ([Wharton](https://statistics.mbacareers.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2025-Career-Report.pdf), [Haas](https://haas.berkeley.edu/recruiters/report/))
- **Base only.** These medians exclude signing bonuses and equity, and tech offers often include both. Kellogg's tech median signing bonus was $40,000 ([Kellogg](https://www.kellogg.northwestern.edu/companies-recruiters/recruiter-employment-outcomes/)), Tuck's was $55,000 ([Tuck](https://tuck.dartmouth.edu/mba/career-services/employment-statistics)), and 73.8% of UCLA tech hires received stock or equity ([UCLA](https://www.anderson.ucla.edu/sites/default/files/document/2026-03/Class%20of%202025%20Employment%20Report%20PDF_0.pdf)).
- **Internship pay units differ.** Yale and Stern report *weekly* pay; everyone else reports monthly. The internship classes also differ (see the class shown in each cell).
- **Small-n caution:** Yale tech is 8.3% of hires, and Tepper's sub-industries rest on a handful of students.
- **Context:** Haas says tech hiring rose to 38% of its 2025 class, from 25% in 2024 ([Haas News](https://newsroom.haas.berkeley.edu/record-high-starting-compensation-for-2025-ftmba-grads-fueled-by-tech-sector-rebound/)). Darden says tech hires nearly doubled, from 26 students in 2024 to 47 in 2025 ([Darden News](https://news.darden.virginia.edu/articles/2025-26-employment-results-highlight-remarkable-job-market-success-of-darden-graduates)).
