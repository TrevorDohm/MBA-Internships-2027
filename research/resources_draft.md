### Career-services (CSO) recruiting-policy norms
- Schools restrict employer contact with first-years early in the fall. Stanford GSB's Academic Adjustment Period for the Class of 2028 runs **Sep 10 – Oct 29, 2026**. During it, recruiters may not cold-contact first-years. ([Stanford GSB recruiting policies](https://gsb.stanford.edu/organizations/recruit/strategies-resources/policies))
- Older examples of the same pattern: Wharton (2015–16) allowed no first-year recruiting events before Oct 19, and MIT Sloan (2019) required group-format first-year info sessions in the fall. ([Wharton 2015–16 guide](https://recruiters-corp.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/2015-16-Recruiters-Guide.pdf), [MIT Sloan 2019 policy](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/sites/default/files/2019-04/MIT%20Sloan%20Recruiting%20Policies_FINAL_April%2017%202019.pdf))
- Offer-deadline rules differ by school. For example, UC Davis requires first-year offers to stay open at least two weeks. ([UC Davis GSM](https://gsm.ucdavis.edu/recruit-and-hire-our-students))
- Employment reports at most schools follow the **MBA CSEA Standards** (e.g., Haas). ([Haas recruiters report page](https://haas.berkeley.edu/recruiters/report)). Exact embargo dates and the CSEA/CSO Alliance's own published text: `null` (not retrieved).
